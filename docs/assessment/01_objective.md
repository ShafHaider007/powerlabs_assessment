# 1. Objective

> Source: `docs/Technical Assessment.pdf`, Section 1 (wording copied from the PDF)

**Title:** Technical Assessment: 3D Roof Reconstruction & Solar Panel Placement
**Role:** Geospatial Software Engineer (Computational Geometry & Python)

## From the PDF
> Using the provided data, develop a solution, preferably in Python, to reconstruct rooftop
> geometry and generate a viable solar panel layout.
>
> This assessment is intended to evaluate your problem-solving ability, computational geometry
> skills, algorithm design, and practical implementation.

## In short
- **Input:** a DSM and an RGB aerial image of one building.
- **Output:** (a) a reconstructed rooftop geometry and (b) a viable solar panel layout on it.
- **Language:** Python (preferred).
- **What is being tested:** problem-solving, computational geometry, algorithm design, practical implementation.
